BackgroundsLive WebGL scene · four acts

One live implementation — the page itself: particle-scene.tsx (three.js, bloom + wipe passes, embedded point clouds) under hero-lights.tsx (three PNG light layers, overlay / plus-lighter) and grain.tsx. Each window flies the background of this whole view to one of the page's own scroll stations through its Lenis instance; the scrim lifts while this group is in view.
