Foundations(01)

Typography

One family throughout: Inter from Google Fonts (weights 300–700, mirrored locally), set on body with the page's system fallback stack. Hierarchy comes from weight 600 plus tracking-tight; everything secondary is text-stone-500. Chart ticks use Chart.js' default 12px at weight 500 in #e7e5e4.
